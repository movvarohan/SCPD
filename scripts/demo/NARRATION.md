# SC Sourcing Engine — Demo Narration Script

A voiceover script timed to `sc-sourcing-engine-demo.mp4` (~10 min). The video is
fully self-explanatory with on-screen captions; this script is for an optional
human or AI voiceover. Tone: calm, confident, professional. Pacing: unhurried.

---

**[01 · Getting started]**
Welcome to the SC Sourcing Engine — Stanford Consulting's tool for finding the
right people to email, researching them, writing the outreach, sending it,
following up, and routing booked calls to a PD. Most of it runs on autopilot, and
you stay in control of every guardrail. The first person on the team creates the
workspace by clicking "Create an admin account," entering a name, email, and
password — and they're signed straight in.

**[02 · The Dashboard]**
You land on the dashboard — your whole pipeline at a glance. Total leads,
high-priority, drafts, what's pending review, sent, replies, and booked calls.
Notice the banner: automation is on out of the box. The engine drafts and sends
for you, and that one button pauses everything instantly if you ever need to.

**[03 · Connect your keys & inbox]**
First-time setup lives in Settings. Paste your keys once — Anthropic for the AI,
Apollo for sourcing, Hunter for emails, Tavily for research — each with a test
button. Then connect a sending inbox: Gmail to send as yourself, or Resend over
HTTPS with no personal inbox at all. Until a mailbox is connected, sends are
safely simulated, so nothing goes out before you're ready.

**[04 · Invite your team]**
Bring in the team from Settings. Enter an email, pick a role — Admin, Reviewer,
or PD — and click Invite. You get a one-time join link that expires in fourteen
days. They set a password and land in the app with exactly the access they need.

**[05 · Import your contacts]**
Now get leads in. Upload your alumni spreadsheet or any CSV from Apollo, Clay, or
a conference list. Columns are auto-detected, even messy ones. You preview every
row with validation, then import — and duplicates are caught automatically on
email, LinkedIn, and name-plus-company.

**[06 · Source brand-new leads]**
No list yet? Describe your ideal client and pull fresh leads from twelve
public-data connectors — SEC filings and recent raises, IRS 990s, the federal
health registry, NIH, the FDA, federal spending, and more. Real companies, named
executives. None publish emails — and that's fine, because enrichment is next.

**[07 · The Lead Database]**
Every lead lives here. Search and filter instantly — by status, source, score,
alumni connection, industry, or PD. One click runs bulk actions: research your
top leads, enrich missing emails, verify them, or export to CSV.

**[08 · Inside a lead]**
Open any lead for the full picture. Find a real, verified email in a click. The
score is completely transparent — every point is explained. And this is what
makes a cold email land: grounded research that reads the company's own website
and public news — never LinkedIn — and pulls one specific, true detail to
reference, with sources you can check.

**[09 · Generate the outreach]**
Pick your leads, set the type, goal, and tone, and Claude writes a first email
plus two follow-ups — from real, stored facts only. Before you click, a preview
shows how many will send immediately under your rules and how many wait for
review. Every draft is lint-checked: broken personalization or an empty subject
can never auto-send.

**[10 · The Review Queue]**
With automation on, this is the exceptions desk — it only collects what
shouldn't auto-send: a missing email, a warning, a rule miss. You see why each
lead was chosen, the personalization, the confidence, and any flags. Approve and
it sends from your own inbox.

**[11 · Automation rules]**
Decide what sends on its own — by industry, company size, role, and a minimum
score. Keep the guardrails on: verified emails only, skip drafts with warnings,
and a daily cap. And the send window means automated email only goes out during
business hours in your timezone — so it reads like a person, not a bot.

**[12 · Automatic follow-ups]**
Follow-up one on day three, follow-up two on day seven — configurable, and
hands-off. Before every run it checks the inbox first, so anyone who replied,
booked, or opted out never gets another message.

**[13 · The do-not-contact list]**
This is the hardest guarantee in the product. Anyone who replies "unsubscribe"
lands here automatically and is never emailed again, by any path — even if
they're re-imported later. You can add an address by hand too. Removals are
admin-only, and everything is audited.

**[14 · Sending & Tracking]**
Track every outbound — ready, sent, replied, booked. Replies sync from your
inbox, opt-outs are honored automatically, and a compliant footer is added on
every send.

**[15 · Analytics]**
Step back and see the whole picture: sends and replies week by week, the funnel
from lead to booked call with conversion at every stage, and which sources and
industries produce your best leads.

**[16 · Route & tune]**
When a call books, route it to the right PD — matched on sector, function,
availability, and load, with a plain-English reason. And tune what "a good lead"
means by weighting each scoring rule, then re-score the whole database at once.

**[Outro]**
That's the whole loop: sign up, set up keys, invite the team, import and source,
enrich and research, draft, review, send, follow up, track, and assign — most of
it automatic, all of it under your control. Source smarter. Stay human.
