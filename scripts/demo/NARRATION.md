# SC Sourcing Engine — Demo Narration Script

A ~5.5-minute voiceover script timed to `sc-sourcing-engine-demo.mp4`. The video
is fully self-explanatory with on-screen captions; this script is for an
optional human or AI voiceover. Tone: calm, confident, professional. Pacing:
unhurried.

---

**[Intro — title]**
Welcome to the SC Sourcing Engine — Stanford Consulting's tool for finding,
researching, and reaching out to the right people, with a human in the loop.

**[What it does]**
The hardest part of sourcing isn't the call — it's getting the first call. This
tool automates the annoying pre-call work: finding leads, enriching them,
scoring them, researching them, and drafting outreach. But it never sends
anything without a person approving it first.

**[The pipeline]**
Here's the whole flow: source, dedupe, score, enrich the email, research, and
draft. Then a human reviews it, it sends from your own inbox, replies are
tracked, and booked calls are assigned to a PD. Twelve public datasets feed it,
alongside your alumni spreadsheet and Apollo.

**[01 Dashboard]**
You land on the dashboard — your whole pipeline at a glance. Total leads,
high-priority, drafts, what's pending review, what's been sent, replies, and
booked calls. The charts update live as you work.

**[02 Import]**
Start by importing. Upload your alumni spreadsheet, or any lead CSV. Columns are
auto-detected — even messy ones. You confirm the mapping, preview the rows with
validation, and import. Duplicates are caught automatically on email, LinkedIn,
and name-plus-company.

**[03 Source Leads]**
Need more leads? Define your ideal customer profile and pull from twelve
public-data connectors — SEC filings, IRS 990s, the federal health registry,
NIH, the FDA, federal spending, and more. These are real companies and named
executives that most teams never touch. None of them publish emails — and that's
fine, because enrichment is next.

**[04 Lead Database]**
Every lead lives here. Search and filter instantly — by status, source, score,
alumni connection, industry, or PD. And one click runs bulk actions: research
your top leads, enrich missing emails, verify them, or export to CSV.

**[05 Lead Detail & Research]**
Open any lead for the full picture. The lead score is completely transparent —
every point is explained. And this is the part that matters: grounded research.
It reads the company's own website and public news — never LinkedIn — and pulls
one specific, true detail your email can reference, with sources you can check.

**[06 Outreach Generator]**
Now the outreach. Pick your leads, choose the email type, goal, and tone, and
Claude writes the first email plus two follow-ups — using only real, stored
facts. It never invents relationships or clients. Everything goes to the review
queue. Nothing sends automatically.

**[07 Review Queue]**
This is the safety gate. Every draft shows why the lead was chosen, what
personalization it used, a confidence score, and any warnings. You edit,
approve, regenerate, or reject. "Approve and send" delivers it as your own inbox.

**[08 Sending & Tracking]**
Track every outbound — ready, sent, replied, booked. Replies sync from your
inbox, opt-outs are honored automatically, and a compliant footer is added on
send.

**[09 PD Assignments]**
When a call books, route it to the right PD — matched on sector, function,
availability, and current load, with a plain-English explanation for the
recommendation.

**[10 Settings & Keys]**
Configure everything here: your org identity, the claims you allow, scoring
weights, and templates. Connect your keys and your inbox — each with a test
button — and you're sending for real.

**[Recap / Setup / Outro]**
That's the whole loop: source, enrich, research, draft, review, send, track, and
assign. To run it: npm install, copy the env file, npm run setup, and npm run
dev. It works offline with mock data — add your keys when you're ready. Source
smarter. Stay human.
